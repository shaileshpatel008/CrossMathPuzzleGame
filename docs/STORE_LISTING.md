## Full description (max 4000) — 2171 chars
```
Love Sudoku, crosswords or number games? CrossMath Puzzle combines them into one relaxing, addictive brain teaser. Every row and every column is a math equation — fill the empty cells with the right numbers so that all of them are true!

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap an empty cell, then tap a number from the tray
• Every row and column must form a correct equation
• Each number tile is used exactly once
• Three mistakes and the level ends — think before you tap!
• In longer equations, multiply and divide before you add and subtract

FROM EASY TO MASTER
Start with simple addition and subtraction and work your way up to big 13×13 grids with all four operations, long equations and tricky decoy tiles. Thousands of levels, each with exactly one correct solution, and the difficulty grows as you play:
• Easy – quick warm-ups with + and −
• Medium – multiplication joins in
• Hard – all four operations on a 9×9 grid
• Expert – long equations and decoy numbers
• Master – the ultimate test, with only two mistakes allowed

DAILY CHALLENGE
A fresh puzzle every day — the same one for every player. Keep your streak alive, solve 25 dailies in a month and add a shiny trophy to your cabinet.

FEATURES
• Journey mode with chapters, stars and rewards
• Quick Play: endless puzzles in any difficulty
• Hints, undo, erase and auto-check when you need help
• Daily gift with bigger rewards every day
• 14 awards to unlock and detailed statistics
• 8 beautiful board themes, including a light Paper theme
• Adjustable number size, sound, music and vibration
• Play offline — no internet needed to solve puzzles
• Your progress is saved automatically

GREAT FOR YOUR BRAIN
Math puzzles are a fun way to sharpen mental arithmetic, logic and concentration. Play a quick level on the bus or relax with a long Master puzzle in the evening.

CrossMath Puzzle is free to play with ads. Optional in-app purchases let you remove ads, buy hint packs or join VIP for no ads, unlimited hints, Master mode, every past daily challenge and exclusive themes. VIP is an auto-renewing subscription that you can cancel anytime in Google Play.

Download CrossMath Puzzle and start training your brain today!
```
