This policy explains what information the CrossMath Puzzle app ("the app", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by [DEVELOPER NAME]. Questions: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Summary

  • You do not create an account, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress, settings and statistics are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising identifier.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Levels, stars, hints, daily-challenge results, achievements, statistics and settings (sound, theme, reminder time) are saved locally on your device so you can continue playing. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings › Reset all progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interactions.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy options (ads consent).
  • On iOS, Apple's tracking prompt lets you allow or deny tracking.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Buying Remove Ads or VIP turns off banner, full-screen, app-open and native ads. Optional reward videos, which you choose to watch, remain available.

In-app purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads, hint packs, VIP subscriptions) are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store under their own privacy policies. The app receives only the confirmation that a product was bought so it can unlock it, and checks with the store to restore your purchases. We do not receive your name, e-mail or payment information. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Google Play › Subscriptions or in your Apple ID settings.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules a local daily-challenge reminder on your device. No data is sent for this, and you can turn it off in Settings or in your device's notification settings.
